# Pricing and packaging: Gling, Descript, Captions (Mirage), Submagic (checked 4 November 2026)

How to read this file:

- **Status labels.** `official page` = read today on the vendor's own site. `third-party` = not the vendor. `calculated` = my arithmetic from official figures. `estimate` = my inference, not stated by anyone. `not verified` = could not be confirmed.
- **How pages were read.** I checked the main Captions, Submagic and Descript price cards and the Gling Plus and Pro cards against the raw HTML. Help-centre figures, and Gling's annual and Elite figures, came through a page-reading tool and were not separately checked against raw HTML.
- **Currency.** All prices are USD unless noted. Gling and Descript use "$" with no currency code, so USD is assumed for those two.
- **Region.** The pages were fetched from a server, not a browser in Thailand, so a Thai visitor may see different prices.

### Inferences

**Cost of one 10-minute talking-head video**

- **Gling.** No specific figure is stated. `estimate` It uses 10 minutes of the hour quota, assuming the meter counts uploaded footage; the page says "AI edited media" — [Gling pricing](https://www.gling.ai/pricing).
- **Descript.**
  - Uploading or recording counts as media minutes, so about 10 minutes — [Descript usage help](https://help.descript.com/billing-payments-plans/track-and-understand-your-media-minutes-and-ai-credits).
  - AI credits scale with job size. Studio Sound and Remove Filler Words each cap at 30 credits per file or application. Underlord has "no fixed price per action" — same source.

## Features gated by plan, and the visible reason to upgrade

### Takeaway
The upgrade trigger differs by vendor: Gling sells volume and support, Descript sells 4K plus custom fonts plus top-ups at Creator, Captions sells generative AI at Max, and Submagic sells longer videos plus AI editing at Pro.

### Cited Findings
- **Gling:** the feature checklist is the same on every tier. Stated differences are hours, watermark and Premium support, which starts at Pro — [Gling pricing](https://www.gling.ai/pricing).
- **Descript:**
  - Hobbyist adds watermark removal and 1080p — [Descript pricing](https://www.descript.com/pricing).
  - Creator adds 4K, custom fonts, top-ups, unlimited stock search and advanced formats — same source.
  - Business adds Brand Studio team-wide, translation proofreading, native-sounding AI speakers and priority support — same source.
- **Captions:**
  - Basic adds Reddit to Video, Script Generator, Teleprompter, Caption & Dub, Eye Contact, Denoise and Censor — [Captions plans help](https://captions.ai/help/docs/subscriptions).
  - Max adds Chat to Edit, AI Edit, Clips, generated actors and up to 30 AI Twins — same source.
  - Frontier adds "our most sophisticated tier of generative AI models", more usage and faster generation — [Captions pricing](https://www.captions.ai/pricing); [Captions plans help](https://captions.ai/help/docs/subscriptions).
- **Submagic:**
  - Starter is limited to AI Auto Captions, free b-roll, text-based trimming and 1080p. Its table shows "-" for hook titles, silence removal, filler words, clean audio and eye correction — [Submagic pricing](https://www.submagic.co/pricing).
  - Pro adds 5-minute videos, Storyblocks b-roll, auto zooms, remove bad takes and silences, clean audio, publishing and Brand Kit — same source.
  - Business adds 30-minute videos, 4K at 60 FPS, logos and brand assets, custom dictionary, and priority support and rendering — same source.
  - The page contains internal conflicts on Starter features: one plan list includes AI Clean audio and another excludes it, and the card marks are not readable in extracted text — same source.
