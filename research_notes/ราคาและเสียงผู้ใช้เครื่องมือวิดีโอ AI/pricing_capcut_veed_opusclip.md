## What is the quota unit, what does a 10-minute video cost, and do units roll over?

### Takeaway
Only OpusClip charges by source minutes: a 10-minute video costs 10 credits. VEED's editing tools and subtitles are not metered by a stated credit cost, and its credits pay for AI generation. CapCut meters AI credits and publishes no cost for a talking-head edit.

### Cited Findings

| Item | CapCut | VEED | OpusClip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unit | AI credits | AI credits, plus subtitle hours and dubbing allowances | Credits; 1 credit = 1 minute of video processing |
| Sold per | account; seat on Teams | editor seat | account; packs on Pro |
| Rollover | not stated | no: "Credits do not carry over" | monthly plan: credits last 60 days; yearly plan: 12 months |
| More units | "Buy credits" listed on paid plans; price not shown | move to a higher credit allowance; no separate top-up described | Pro: "On-demand credit top-ups", marked New; price not shown |
| 10-min talking-head cost | not stated | no stated cost for editing | 10 credits |

Sources: [CapCut pricing](https://www.capcut.com/pricing), [VEED pricing](https://www.veed.io/pricing/index.html.md), [OpusClip pricing](https://www.opus.pro/pricing), [OpusClip credits help](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/how-are-credits-consumed)

**OpusClip rules [official]** — [credits help](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/how-are-credits-consumed):
- "1 credit per minute of the original video imported". Partial minutes round down; videos under a minute cost 1.
- X posts cost 3 credits per clip, per X account; [Castmagic, verified 20 Aug 2026](https://www.castmagic.io/blog/opus-clip-pricing) says 1, so the help page's figure stands.
- Yearly plans receive the full year's credits upfront, valid 12 months.
- Team credits are shared among members.

**OpusClip conflicts on its own pages [official vs official]:**
- Pro rollover: the [pricing page](https://www.opus.pro/pricing) script says 1 month on monthly and "2 months" on yearly; [help](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/how-are-credits-consumed) says yearly credits last 12 months.
- Credits after cancelling: [credits help](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/how-are-credits-consumed) says they expire at their original expiry date; [Where did my minutes go](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/where-did-my-minutes-go) says at the end of the current billing cycle.
- Top-ups: the [pricing page](https://www.opus.pro/pricing) lists on-demand top-ups; [Castmagic, 20 Aug 2026](https://www.castmagic.io/blog/opus-clip-pricing) [third-party] says credits cannot be bought alone, only packs or a tier upgrade. The top-up line is marked New, so Castmagic may predate it.

**VEED rules [official]** — [VEED pricing](https://www.veed.io/pricing/index.html.md):
- Subtitle generation is unlimited on paid plans, under fair use of 50 hours/month or 600 hours/year. Free gets 10 min/month.
- Fabric video costs 8 credits per second at 720p — [VEED pricing index](https://www.veed.io/pricing/llms.txt).
- A 30-minute translation uses 30 minutes, again for each further language. Unused minutes do not roll over.
- Conflict on the same page: the feature table says subtitle translation is "Translate with Credits", while the FAQ says translations are minute-based. It states no per-plan translation allowance.
- Dubbing allowance: Pro 6 hr/yr or 30 min/month; Studio 12 hr/yr or 1 hr/month; Free and Creator "Try once".
- AI voice: Free 10 min/month; Creator 4 hr/yr or 2 hr/month; Pro 144 hr/yr or 12 hr/month; Studio 960 hr/yr or 80 hr/month.
- API is separate: API credits are prepaid and editor credits do not pay for API jobs — [third-party, Magic Hour, checked Sep 2026](https://magichour.ai/blog/veed-pricing). VEED's index confirms separate API plans and per-model pricing — [official](https://www.veed.io/pricing/llms.txt).

**CapCut rules [official]** — [CapCut pricing](https://www.capcut.com/pricing):
- Seedance 2.5 from $0.12/sec on Pro and $0.18/sec on Ultra; Seedance 2.0 Mini at 480p from $0.007/sec on Pro and $0.009/sec on Ultra. Pro and Ultra rates are marked "(first month)". Ultra's listed rates are higher than Pro's.
- Generation length: Pro up to 240s at 720p or 600s at 480p; Ultra up to 1,700s at 720p or 4,300s at 480p, with Seedance 2.0 Mini.
- These are the only quota figures on the page, and they cover AI generation, not editing.

### Inferences
- [calc] OpusClip 10-minute videos per month: Free 6, Starter 15, Pro 30 per pack.
- [calc] Generating 10 minutes of VEED Fabric video would cost 4,800 credits (600 s x 8). That is AI avatar generation, not editing a filmed talking head, so it should not be used as VEED's cost for Komoray's use case.
- [calc] OpusClip's cost per 10 minutes processed in Tier 1: Starter $1.00; Pro monthly $0.97; Pro yearly $0.48.

### Gaps
- OpusClip free-credit rollover is not stated on any page I read.
- VEED and CapCut do not state a credit cost for a 10-minute talking-head edit. Whether VEED's Remove Silences, Filler Words or Clips draw credits is not stated; the table shows only "Yes".
- Top-up prices for CapCut and OpusClip are not public on the pages read.

## Which features are gated, and what is the visible reason to upgrade?

### Takeaway
The first upgrade is about removing the watermark everywhere. The second is about AI output and professional finishing: 4K and brand tools at VEED, B-roll, dubbing and NLE export at OpusClip, AI credits and premium assets at CapCut.

### Cited Findings
- VEED Free to Creator: no watermark, 1080p, unlimited subtitles, stock catalog, iOS app; AI tools move from "Try once" to included (Remove Silences, Remove Filler Words, Clean Audio, Eye Contact, Clips, AI B-roll, Auto-edits) — [VEED pricing](https://www.veed.io/pricing/index.html.md)
- VEED Creator to Pro: 4K, 50GB, unlimited subtitle styles, multiple brand kits, custom vocabulary, custom templates, embeds, dubbing allowance, Text Behind Person — [VEED pricing](https://www.veed.io/pricing/index.html.md)
- VEED Pro to Studio: unlimited storage, custom watermarks, 5 editors, 1 reviewer, branded hosting, more credits and AI voice — [VEED pricing](https://www.veed.io/pricing/index.html.md)
- OpusClip Free to Starter: "Remove Watermark", "Powerful editor", virality score, filler and silence removal, faster processing, 30-day export window; AI voice-over 20/day, AI image and stock B-roll 3 clips/month each — [OpusClip pricing](https://www.opus.pro/pricing)
- OpusClip Starter to Pro: AI B-roll, 10+ import sources, Premiere and DaVinci export, multiple aspect ratios, scheduler, top-ups, custom fonts, speech enhancement, limited API, dubbing, team workspace with 2 seats — [OpusClip pricing](https://www.opus.pro/pricing)
- OpusClip Pro to Business: priority processing, customised seats and credits, dedicated storage, API, MSA, priority Slack support — [OpusClip pricing](https://www.opus.pro/pricing)
- CapCut Free to Pro: AI features, all premium templates, premium Pro features, 1 TB, 1,300 credits; the page's headline for Pro and Ultra is AI generation length, not editing — [CapCut pricing](https://www.capcut.com/pricing)
- CapCut Pro to Ultra: "exclusive AI features" and 28,371 credits — [CapCut pricing](https://www.capcut.com/pricing). The FAQ describes Ultra as for "more demanding creative workflows", same source.
- CapCut individual to Teams: brand kit, Pro features for all seats — [CapCut pricing](https://www.capcut.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- VEED gives Remove Silences and Remove Filler Words at its lowest paid tier, and OpusClip at Starter. For the talking-head workflow, silence and filler removal is table stakes rather than a premium feature.
- CapCut's pricing page now sells generation seconds of Seedance before it sells editing. That leaves room for a positioning aimed at people who film themselves.
- OpusClip's NLE export at Pro, and VEED's custom vocabulary at Pro, are the gates closest to professional need. Custom vocabulary is relevant to Komoray's planned personal dictionary.

### Gaps
- CapCut's plan-level feature table behind "See all benefits" was not captured, so gating of captions, auto-cut and resolution is not confirmed officially.

## What are the refund and cancellation terms, and what changed in 2025 to 2026?

### Takeaway
All three auto-renew and generally refuse refunds after use. CapCut's 2026 price restructure is the major change, and it was largely unannounced: CapCut's help pages admit upgraded plans and pricing but give no amounts or dates. Its June 2025 terms controversy concerns a user-content license that is still in the April 2026 terms.

### Cited Findings

**Refund and cancellation [official]:**

| Item | CapCut | VEED | OpusClip |

## Reference points: Riverside, TimeBolt and Recut pricing models

### Takeaway
Riverside is purely subscription. TimeBolt and Recut both offer a one-time purchase alongside a subscription, which shows that a perpetual licence is viable where the tool runs on the user's own computer.

### Cited Findings
**Riverside** is a subscription model, billed monthly or annually. Free is $0 with a one-off 2 hours of recording. Pro is $29/month or $24/month billed $288/year, with 5 hours/month and 2,000 starting AI credits. Grow, labelled "Best Value", is $39 or $34 billed $408/year, with 15 hours/month. Webinar is $99 or $79, with unlimited recording. Business is custom, with 12,000 starting AI credits. Extra AI credits can be bought. The page is inconsistent: it shows "Billed $408 annually" against Webinar, where $79/month would be $948/year [calc], and its FAQ says recording is unlimited with only separate-track downloads capped — [Riverside pricing](https://riverside.com/pricing). Caution: the reader tool flagged the page as a possible staging copy. A direct fetch of riverside.com/pricing returned 200 and I found "staging" only in asset names and a host check, so I treat it as live, but these figures got one read only.

**TimeBolt** sells both models. Pro Monthly is $17/month, Pro Annual is $97/year (stated saving 52%), and Pro Lifetime is $347 once with all future updates, though its sharing feature is capped at 4 minutes and watermarked. Free is permanent: video only, silence removal, watermarked exports, no XML export. Teams is $97/seat/year; Vault is custom. Filler-word removal (UMCHECK) is a pay-per-use add-on at $0.03 per minute of video. One licence covers 2 machines, and there is a 30-day money-back guarantee — [TimeBolt pricing](https://timebolt.io/pricing).

**Recut** also sells both: $129 one-time, or $15/month with an annual option labelled "56% less" whose price I could not see. It includes silence removal, export to major editors, multi-track and one user, with a 30-day money-back guarantee. The free trial is fully functional, unwatermarked, gives 3 free exports and needs no card — [Recut](https://getrecut.com/).

### Inferences
- TimeBolt's separate $0.03/minute charge is the only per-minute price for a specific AI edit found in this research, and a useful benchmark for metering a costly step such as filler-word detection: [calc] $0.30 for a 10-minute video.
- One-time pricing works for these two because they are desktop tools with low server cost. This is my reasoning from the plans listed, not a vendor statement. Komoray pays for Deepgram, Claude and Modal per video, so a lifetime plan carries a cost risk that they largely do not.

### Gaps
- Recut's annual price. Riverside's credit cost per feature and its top-up price.
