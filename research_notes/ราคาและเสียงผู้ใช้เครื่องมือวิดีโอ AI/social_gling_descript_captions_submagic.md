# Social listening: what users say about Gling, Descript, Captions (Mirage) and Submagic (as of 4 Nov 2026)

**Read this first: what the evidence is, and is not.**

- **Almost everything direct comes from one platform, Trustpilot, plus one Descript feature-request board (Canny).** Theme counts below are distinct reviewers on the pages I saw. They are not prevalence figures. "Across independent sources" is mostly one source, and the tables say so.
- **Blocked:** Reddit (WebFetch: "unable to fetch from www.reddit.com"; WebSearch rejects the domain). G2 and Capterra returned HTTP 403. Product Hunt URLs I tried returned 404. **I have no first-hand Reddit quote for any tool.** Reddit items here are second-hand and labelled.
- **Not covered:** YouTube review videos and comments, X, LinkedIn. Hacker News searches returned only one relevant item, and it is by Gling's founder, so it is not user voice.
- **Pages viewed, so counts are floors:**
  - Descript Trustpilot: default page, 1-star page 2, 5-star page 1.
  - Captions Trustpilot: default page, page 2, 1-star page 1 of about 5.
  - Submagic Trustpilot: default page, 1-star page 1.
  - Gling Trustpilot: all 3 reviews.
  - Star-filtered pages inflate whichever star they filter on, so do not compare a praise count with a complaint count as a ratio.
- **Quote reliability:**
  - Quotes reached me through a fetch tool that summarises pages. It returned excerpts, usually capped at 125 characters, so many are fragments of longer reviews.
  - I could not get per-review permalinks, so links go to the review listing where the review appeared.
  - **Re-check any quote against the live page before using it in marketing.** The wording below is as returned. I have not seen the raw page.
  - Reviewer names are for attribution in these notes. Do not republish them without permission.
- **Labels:** [DIRECT] = fetched page. [SNIPPET] = search result summary only, not seen on the page. [SECOND-HAND] = another site reporting it. [AFFILIATE] and [VENDOR] = discount these.
- **Platform bias:**
  - Trustpilot's complaint skew and G2's incentivised reviews are taken from the brief. I did not research them separately.
  - Gling and Captions Trustpilot pages state they have not invited customers (recently, for Captions), and Trustpilot notes it does not fact-check reviews ([Captions p.2](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/captions.ai?page=2), [Gling](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/gling.ai)).

## For each tool: top praise and top complaints, ranked by recurrence

### Takeaway
Descript is the only tool here with a clearly negative public record: TrustScore 2.6, driven by billing friction, then AI credit burn and bugs. Captions and Submagic both score 4.5, but much of that praise is for support staff, and several of those 5-star reviews are about fixing a billing problem. Gling has almost no public review record (3 Trustpilot reviews), so no ranking is defensible for it.

## What pushes people to cancel or switch, and to which tool?

### Takeaway
Stated triggers are clear: credit burn with poor output, billing surprises and bugs. Destination is mostly unstated. CapCut is the only destination named more than once in what I could read (2 reviewers), and neither names Gling, Submagic or Captions. Treat "who they switch to" as unanswered.

### Cited Findings
- **Credit burn leading to cancellation:** on the $35/month Creator plan, one user used all 800 credits in two days while processing only 12 of 1,800 media minutes, with output needing heavy manual fixing, and cancelled (fetch summary; the Underlord quote above is the verbatim part) (Evgueniya Korabliova, 15 Jun 2026) — [Canny](https://descript.canny.io/feature-requests/p/all-ai-monthly-credits-are-gone-after-one-edit-on-creator-plan)
- **Same pattern, one video:** "I purchased the Creator membership with 800 credits, only to successfully complete 1 video." (Michael E, 19 Sep 2026) — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/descript.com); "After editing just one video all my monthly credits are gone." (Sergei Romanov, 11 Feb 2026) — [Canny](https://descript.canny.io/feature-requests/p/all-ai-monthly-credits-are-gone-after-one-edit-on-creator-plan)
- **Named destination, CapCut:** "Overall, your better of using CapCut" (Philip ten Brink, 7 Jun 2026); "I should have kept with CapCut" (Valerie Adjorlolo, 22 Apr 2026) — [Trustpilot 1-star p.2](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/descript.com?stars=1&page=2)
- **Named destination, Riverside [SECOND-HAND, one anecdote]:** an r/podcasting user reportedly moved a two-person show to Riverside in Feb 2026 and found renders slower — [nicheof.one](https://nicheof.one/feed/descript-review-pricing-ai-credits-2026/)
- **Reliability that risks a channel:** "Use Descript at your own risk if you plan to publish to YouTube." (Nathan McKelvey, 30 Mar 2026; excerpt only, the reason is not visible) — [Trustpilot 1-star p.2](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/descript.com?stars=1&page=2)
- **Narrow refund windows:** "Couldn't get a refund because they have a 48h refund policy" (Andreas, 28 May 2026) — [Trustpilot 1-star p.2](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/descript.com?stars=1&page=2); "they do not give refunds for any of the monthly subscriptions" (Brendon, 7 Oct 2026) — [Captions](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/captions.ai?stars=1); "they don't do refunds of any kind." (Roberto Escapita, 20 Aug 2025) — [Submagic](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/submagic.co?stars=1)
- **Official cancel paths do exist, which contradicts "impossible":**
  - Descript: App Settings, Plan tab, and a cancellation reason is required [SNIPPET] — [Descript help](https://help.descript.com/billing-payments-plans/cancel)
  - Captions: Settings, Billing, Manage Plan, Cancel Subscription; monthly plans are non-refundable [SNIPPET] — [Captions help](https://captions.ai/help/mirage/account-management/mirage-billing)
- **Deleting the app or account does not stop billing, a recurring trap:** "I had an issue with continued billing after deleting my account" (Kaimyn Eddie, 3 Nov 2026) — [Captions](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/captions.ai)
- **Non-billing exit, Gling:** a user left because their workflow changed, and would return: "once life goes back to normal I wouldn't hesitate to sign up again" (Adrian S, 15 Jun 2026) — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/gling.ai)
- **Non-English accuracy as a cancel reason:** I found no such case on any tool.

### Inferences
- Cancellation may be findable but still feel hidden. The gap between "you can't cancel it" reviews and a documented path suggests a design problem rather than absence (no usability test behind this).
- CapCut as the comparison point is weak evidence, but it fits Komoray's existing wish to avoid being compared with CapCut. The people who said this look like casual users, not professional creators. That is inferred from thin excerpts.
- **Komoray could differentiate on three visible behaviours:** a renewal notice by email, a visible cancel button that does not require a card update, and never charging for failed jobs. Each answers a complaint quoted above.

### Gaps
- Switching destinations would be best read from Reddit, which is blocked. Count: 2 CapCut, 1 second-hand Riverside. No switching evidence for Captions, Submagic or Gling users.
- No churn figures from any vendor.

## How do people feel about credit-based pricing versus flat minutes, and what feels fair?

### Takeaway
The anger is not at the idea of metering. It is at unpredictability: costs not shown up front, credits draining faster than the visible allowance, credits taken for failed output, and core features moved behind credits. I found almost no praise of credits, and no one directly comparing credits with flat minutes in those terms, so "flat is preferred" is an inference.

### Cited Findings
- **Costs are not visible:** "I would appreciate a note on how expensive different actions are" (Bianca Hale, 23 Jul 2026). The fetch summary adds that a co-editor used 70+ credits per video and that her filler-word and clarity edits cost 10 credits each — [Canny](https://descript.canny.io/feature-requests/p/all-ai-monthly-credits-are-gone-after-one-edit-on-creator-plan)
- **Two meters that do not line up:** 800 credits gone with 12 of 1,800 media minutes used (Evgueniya Korabliova, 15 Jun 2026) — [Canny](https://descript.canny.io/feature-requests/p/all-ai-monthly-credits-are-gone-after-one-edit-on-creator-plan)
- **Felt as a changed deal:** "they shifted to a nickle-and-dime tokenization approach about a year ago" (Peter Zalewski, 23 Jun 2026) — [Trustpilot 1-star p.2](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/descript.com?stars=1&page=2)
- **Same, from a long-time payer [SNIPPET, page attribution unclear]:** shifted core functionality into an "AI credits" system, "and it's been an awful experience" — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/230702/Descript/reviews/)
- **Paying for failure:** "AI credits were deducted for Avatar generations that never completed."   - Transcription not metered.

### Inferences
- The implied definition of fair, from these complaints:
  1. Know the cost before you click.
  2. No charge when the job fails or the output is unusable.
  3. One meter, not two.
  4. See the result before paying.
  5. Do not move what used to be included behind a new meter.
- A flat-minutes promise with no credits would answer most of the above, if Komoray's actual cost structure allows it. That is a judgement, not something users stated.

### Gaps
- No direct "credits vs flat minutes" debate seen. Reddit and r/SaaS are the likely places, and they are blocked.
- Little on how Submagic and Gling plans are perceived specifically. Gling price data is from one affiliate page: Free, Plus $20/mo or $10 annually, Pro $40 or $20, Elite $100 or $50 — [McDonnell, AFFILIATE](https://mikemcdonnell.co.uk/gling-ai-review)

## Gling specifically: accuracy, YouTuber workflow, XML export, vertical, non-English, repeated wrong words, Thai

### Takeaway
Public user voice on Gling is very thin. The only report of a repeated wrong word comes from one affiliate reviewer, and I found no user complaint about Thai or Asian languages, possibly because Gling's own language list (as seen in a snippet) names no East, South or Southeast Asian language, so there may be few such users. Absence of complaints here is not evidence of good handling.

### Cited Findings
- **Repeated wrong word, the key item for Komoray (single source, [AFFILIATE], with disclosure):** the reviewer says Gling rendered "Wayfront" as "Wavefront", "Every single time.", and that this "is not limited to that one word." Any term "that is not common English is a candidate." (Page dated 30 Aug 2026; the title says a different month) — [McDonnell](https://mikemcdonnell.co.uk/gling-ai-review)
- **Time saved [AFFILIATE]:** editing "used to take me 3 or 4 hours. It now takes minutes plus a short tidy-up."; processing takes "5 to 6 minutes." — [McDonnell](https://mikemcdonnell.co.uk/gling-ai-review)
- **Scope limits [AFFILIATE]:** "No colour, no graphics, no audio mixing. It assembles dialogue. That is the whole scope."; "The export needs a mastering pass before it goes up." — [McDonnell](https://mikemcdonnell.co.uk/gling-ai-review)
- **Workflow [older, Apr 2023, no affiliate disclosure seen]:** "You chuck your raw a-roll take into Gling, tell it to detect silences and bad takes, and hit a button."; "it only completes half the job"; "That 40-minute a-roll edit would have taken me an hour to complete manually" — [Mark Ellis](https://markellis.substack.com/p/gling-transformed-my-business-yesterday)
- **XML export:**
  - "The latter can be exported to Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or Adobe Premiere." (Apr 2023) — [Mark Ellis](https://markellis.substack.com/p/gling-transformed-my-business-yesterday)
  - Gling's FAQ [SNIPPET; direct fetch returned 404]: "You can export an XML from Gling and import it as a timeline directly to Premiere, Final Cut Pro or DaVinci Resolve." — [gling.ai/download](https://www.gling.ai/download)
  - I saw no user praising or complaining about XML quality in 2025 to 2026.
- **Languages [VENDOR, SNIPPET]:** "Gling currently supports videos in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Russian, Italian, Dutch and Hebrew." **Thai is not listed**, nor Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Vietnamese or Hindi. The snippet is undated, and a different snippet says English only, so it is probably older. Check the current page before quoting — [gling.ai/download](https://www.gling.ai/download)
- **Vertical [no evidence]:** the only 9:16 mention I found is on a competitor page, with unclear attribution — [VIDIO](https://www.vidio.ai/fr-FR/alternatives/gling). McDonnell's review does not cover it — [McDonnell](https://mikemcdonnell.co.uk/gling-ai-review)
- **Direction of travel:** Gling's founder posted a Jan 2026 Show HN for turning blog posts and X threads into talking-head videos. This is the vendor, not a user — [HN item](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46793874)
- **Thai or Asian language complaints on any of the four tools:** none found. Submagic's own page claims 99.5% and also 98.8% for Thai, which is [VENDOR] and internally inconsistent [SNIPPET] — [Submagic Thai page](https://www.submagic.co/auto-subtitle-generator/thai).

### Inferences
- If Gling's language list is current, a Thai creator cannot properly use it at all. The opening for Komoray is then not "better than Gling at Thai" but "Gling does not offer Thai", which needs verifying on Gling's live site before any claim is made.
- McDonnell's "Every single time." matches the personal dictionary idea: a tool that repeats the same error wastes the most trust. One affiliate reviewer's anecdote is enough to frame a test, not to claim a market complaint.

### Gaps
- No independent 2025 to 2026 accuracy data for Gling, and no vertical confirmation. A paid hands-on test or Gling's current docs would settle both.

### Inferences
- "Not AI support, real people" is a surprisingly strong signal in an AI-tool category, repeated in the Submagic reviews (Sorizzo; Ganey's "An AI tool backed by real humans who care"). It could suit a small founder-led product such as Komoray.
- None of the people quoted identifies as a professional, already-earning talking-head creator except by implication. The buyers visible here skew toward beginners, marketers and small businesses. So this evidence may not represent Komoray's target customer.

### Gaps
- No independent time-saved sample large enough to average. The vendor numbers are curated and should not be cited as typical.
- Pro creator voices (likely on YouTube, X, r/PartneredYoutube) were not reachable or not searched here.
